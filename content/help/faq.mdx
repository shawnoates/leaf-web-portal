## General

**Is Leaf free?**
Yes! Leaf has a free plan that works great for personal calendars and small groups. We also offer paid plans for organizations with advanced features.

**Do I need an account to RSVP?**
No, you can RSVP to plans via a shared link without creating an account. But creating one lets you see all your calendars in one place.

**Can I use Leaf on my phone?**
Yes! Leaf works on iOS and Android. Download the app or use the mobile website.

## Calendars and Plans

**How many people can I invite to a calendar?**
Unlimited! Invite as many people as you want.

**Can I make a plan private?**
Yes. When creating a plan, you can choose "invite only" so only people you invite can see it.

**Can plans be recurring?**
In the current version, each plan is one-time. For recurring events, create them as separate plans or contact support about custom solutions.

**What happens if I delete a plan?**
The plan is permanently deleted. People won't see it anymore. If it's canceled instead, it stays in history for reference.

## Invitations and Sharing

**How do I share my calendar with someone?**
Go to your calendar and click **Share**. You can send email invites, share a link, or show a QR code.

**Can someone see my calendar without accepting an invite?**
Only if you share a public link. Private calendars require an invitation.

**What if I want to remove someone from my calendar?**
Go to calendar settings, click **Members**, find their name, and click **Remove**.

## Notifications

**Can I turn off all notifications?**
Yes. Go to settings and toggle off notification types, or set quiet hours so you're not interrupted.

**Why didn't I get a notification?**
Check your notification settings—you might have it turned off or set to digest. Also check spam/junk folders if expecting an email.

## RSVPs

**Can I change my RSVP?**
Yes, anytime. Click a different RSVP option to update your response.

**Do I have to RSVP?**
No, but it helps organizers know who's coming. You can also respond with "Maybe" if you're unsure.

## Billing

**How do I upgrade to a paid plan?**
Go to account settings, click **Billing**, and choose your plan.

**Can I cancel my subscription?**
Yes, anytime. Go to billing settings and click **Cancel Subscription**. You keep access until your billing period ends.

**What payment methods do you accept?**
We accept credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover).

## Still Have Questions?

Can't find the answer? Contact our support team at support@joinleaf.com—we're here to help!
