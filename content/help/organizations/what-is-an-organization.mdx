title: What Is an Organization?
description: Learn how organizations work and when to create one.
---

An organization in Leaf is a shared calendar for a group, club, church, business, or community. It lets you manage multiple calendars under one umbrella.

## How Organizations Work

Instead of managing separate calendars, an organization lets you:
- Create multiple calendars (e.g., one for events, one for meetings)
- Share them with members
- Control who can create, edit, or view plans
- Set consistent roles and permissions across calendars

## Personal vs. Organization Calendar

**Personal Calendar**
- Just you
- Good for your own events
- Limited sharing options

**Organization Calendar**
- Multiple members
- Structured roles (admin, organizer, member)
- Better for groups, clubs, or businesses

## When to Use an Organization

Create an organization if:
- You're managing a club, group, or team
- Multiple people need to create and manage events
- You have different groups (departments, teams, circles) that each need their own calendar
- You want consistent branding and permissions

## Examples of Organizations

- Sports leagues and running clubs
- Churches and faith communities
- Apartment buildings or HOAs
- Small businesses and restaurants
- Schools and student groups
- Friend groups that plan regularly
