---
title: Adding and Removing Members
description: Manage who has access to your organization and its calendars.
---

Organizations grow as you add more members. Here's how to manage your team.

## Add a Member

1. Go to your organization settings
2. Click **Members** or **Invite Members**
3. Enter email addresses (one per line or separated by commas)
4. Choose a role (admin, organizer, or member)
5. Click **Send Invites**

They'll receive an email invitation to join.

## What Members Can Do

**Admin**
- Create, edit, or delete calendars
- Add/remove other members
- Change organization settings
- Manage roles and permissions

**Organizer**
- Create and manage events/plans
- See all calendars
- Cannot change organization settings
- Cannot add/remove members

**Member**
- View and RSVP to plans
- Comment and chat
- Cannot create or edit plans (depends on settings)

## Accept or Decline an Invitation

When you receive an invite:
1. Click the link in your email
2. Choose to **Accept** or **Decline**
3. If you accept, you're added to the organization

## Remove a Member

1. Go to organization settings
2. Find the member in the members list
3. Click **Remove** or the trash icon
4. Confirm

They'll lose access to all calendars in that organization.

## Change Someone's Role

1. Go to organization members
2. Click on their name
3. Select a new role
4. Save

They'll immediately get the new permissions.
