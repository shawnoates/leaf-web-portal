---
title: Notification Settings
description: Customize when and how you receive notifications from Leaf.
---

Control your notifications so you stay informed without being overwhelmed.

## Notification Preferences

1. Go to account settings
2. Click **Notifications**
3. You'll see options for each notification type

## By Notification Type

Toggle these on or off:

**Plans**
- New plans created on your calendars
- Plan details changed
- Plan canceled

**RSVPs**
- Someone responded to a plan you're hosting
- Reminders before plans start

**Messages**
- Someone replied in chat
- You were mentioned
- Direct messages

**Calendars**
- Invite to join a calendar
- Member joined your calendar

## Notification Timing

**Immediate**: Get notified right away (default)
**Digest**: Get one email per day with all updates
**Off**: Don't notify for this type

Choose immediate for important updates, digest to reduce interruptions.

## Quiet Hours

Set times when you don't want notifications:
1. Click **Quiet Hours**
2. Set start and end times
3. Choose which days apply
4. Save

Notifications won't bother you during quiet hours, but you'll see them when you return.

## Device Preferences

Notifications can go to:
- **Browser**: If you have Leaf open in a tab
- **Phone**: Leaf mobile app
- **Email**: For important updates
- **SMS**: Text messages (if enabled)

Choose your preferred channels.

## Fine-Tune by Calendar

Need different settings for different calendars?
1. Go to a calendar
2. Click settings
3. Customize notifications just for this calendar

Example: Get immediate alerts for work calendar, digest emails for hobby calendar.
