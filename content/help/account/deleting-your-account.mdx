---
title: Deleting Your Account
description: Learn how to permanently delete your Leaf account.
---

If you decide Leaf isn't for you, we're sorry to see you go. Here's how to delete your account.

## Before You Delete

Before deleting, consider:
- You'll lose access to all your calendars
- Calendars you created will be deleted
- Events you're attending stay (organized by other hosts)
- This action can't be undone
- If you're an admin, transfer your calendars first

## Transfer Ownership

If you're the admin of calendars or organizations:
1. Go to calendar settings
2. Click **Members**
3. Promote another member to admin
4. That person now owns the calendar
5. Then you can safely delete your account

This way, your calendars don't disappear when you leave.

## Delete Your Account

1. Go to account settings
2. Scroll to the bottom
3. Click **Delete Account** or **Danger Zone**
4. Click **Delete My Account**
5. Confirm (you might need to type your email to confirm)

Your account and all your data will be permanently deleted.

## What Happens Next

- You'll be logged out immediately
- All your personal calendars are deleted
- You're removed from other people's calendars
- Your profile and data are erased from our servers
- You can create a new account with the same email later

## Support

If you're having issues with Leaf, we'd love to help before you leave. Contact support@joinleaf.com to discuss.

## Re-activate

Once your account is deleted, you can sign up again with the same email if you change your mind later.
