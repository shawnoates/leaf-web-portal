---
title: Inviting People to Your Calendar
description: Learn how to invite friends, family, or community members to your Leaf calendar.
---

Leaf works best when everyone you want to plan with can see your calendar. Here are the ways to invite people.

## Send Email Invites

1. Go to your calendar settings
2. Click **Invite Members**
3. Enter email addresses (separated by commas for multiple people)
4. Click **Send Invites**

They'll receive an email with a link to join your calendar.

## Share a Link

1. Open your calendar
2. Click **Share** in the top menu
3. Copy the calendar link
4. Share it with people via text, email, or social media

Anyone with the link can view and RSVP to your plans.

## Share via QR Code

1. Click **Share** on your calendar
2. Select **QR Code**
3. Scan the code with a phone camera or QR code reader
4. They'll be taken to your calendar

This works great for in-person events—just show the QR code and people can join immediately.
