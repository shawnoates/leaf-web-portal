---

A calendar in Leaf is where you invite people and manage all your events and plans. Here's how to create one.

## Get Started

1. Sign in to Leaf at [os.joinleaf.com](https://os.joinleaf.com)
2. Click **Create Calendar** on your dashboard
3. Enter a name for your calendar (e.g., "Book Club", "Running Group", "Church Events")
4. Choose whether this is for personal use or an organization
5. Click **Create**

## What's Next

Once your calendar is created, you can:
- Invite people via email or shareable link
- Create events and plans
- Customize calendar settings
- Enable features like RSVPs and notifications

Your calendar is now ready to use. Start inviting people and creating your first plan!
