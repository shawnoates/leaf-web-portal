---
title: Understanding Your Dashboard
description: A tour of the Leaf dashboard and how to navigate your calendars.
---

The dashboard is your home base in Leaf. Here's what you'll find.

## Your Calendars

At the top of your dashboard, you'll see all your calendars. Click on any calendar to view its plans and members.

## Upcoming Plans

The main section shows your upcoming plans across all your calendars, organized by date. Click any plan to RSVP or see details.

## Calendar Settings

Click the gear icon next to a calendar name to:
- Change the calendar name or description
- Manage members and permissions
- Enable or disable features (chat, RSVPs, etc.)
- Set notification preferences

## Search and Filter

Use the search bar to find plans by name, date, or location. Filter by calendar or status to narrow results.

## Quick Actions

Use the buttons in the top right to:
- Create a new plan
- Invite members
- Access your account settings
- View notifications

## Mobile Access

Your dashboard works on phones too. All calendars and plans sync across devices in real-time.
