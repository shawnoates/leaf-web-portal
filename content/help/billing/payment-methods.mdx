title: Payment Methods
description: Add and manage payment methods for your subscription.
---

Leaf accepts credit cards and other payment methods for subscriptions.

## Add a Payment Method

1. Go to account settings
2. Click **Billing** → **Payment Methods**
3. Click **Add Payment Method**
4. Enter your card details (or other payment info)
5. Save

Your payment method is secure and encrypted.

## Supported Payment Methods

- Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover)
- Debit cards
- Other methods depending on your location

## Change Your Payment Method

If your card expires or you want to use a different one:
1. Go to **Payment Methods**
2. Click the card you want to replace
3. Click **Update** or **Change**
4. Enter new payment details
5. Save

Your subscription will be charged to the new card going forward.

## What if Payment Fails?

If a charge fails:
1. Leaf will retry it a few times
2. You'll receive email notifications
3. Update your payment method as soon as possible
4. Your service won't be interrupted during the retry period

## Billing History

To see past charges:
1. Go to **Billing**
2. Click **Billing History** or **Invoices**
3. You can download receipts for tax records

## Questions About Billing?

Contact support@joinleaf.com if you have questions about charges or payment issues.
