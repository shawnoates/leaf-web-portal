Leaf offers free and paid plans. Here's what each includes.

## Free Plan

The Free plan is always available:
- Create and manage calendars
- Invite unlimited people
- Create unlimited plans
- Basic RSVP tracking
- Plan chat
- Mobile app access

Free is perfect for personal calendars and small groups.

## Paid Plans

Paid plans add features for organizations and businesses:
- Advanced member roles and permissions
- Organization branding
- Multiple calendar management
- Advanced analytics
- Priority support
- Integration features

Pricing varies by plan type. Check [joinleaf.com](https://joinleaf.com) for current rates.

## How Billing Works

- Monthly or annual billing (annual gives a discount)
- Billed on the same day each month
- Cancel anytime
- No long-term contracts

## Upgrade to Paid

1. Go to account or organization settings
2. Click **Billing** or **Upgrade**
3. Choose your plan and billing frequency
4. Enter payment information
5. Your subscription starts immediately

You'll have full access to paid features right away.

## View Your Subscription

1. Go to account settings
2. Click **Billing**
3. You'll see:
   - Current plan
   - Next billing date
   - Payment method
   - Billing history
