---
title: Managing RSVPs
description: View and manage who's attending your plans, and send reminders.
---

As a plan host, you can see who's attending, who's declined, and who hasn't responded yet.

## View Attendee List

1. Open your plan
2. Click **Attendees** or **RSVPs**
3. You'll see three groups:
   - **Going**: People who said yes
   - **Maybe**: People who aren't sure
   - **Not going**: People who declined

## Follow Up with No-Responses

1. Next to the attendee count, click **Remind**
2. Select which people to send reminders to (or send to all)
3. Leaf will send them a notification

## Contact an Attendee

Click on any person's name to:
- Send them a direct message
- See their contact info
- Check their availability for other plans

## Adjust Capacity

If you set a max attendee limit and hit it:
1. Open plan settings
2. Increase the max attendees
3. A waitlist (if any) is notified automatically

## Check-in at the Event

When the plan starts:
1. Open the attendee list
2. Click **Check-in** next to each person as they arrive
3. This helps track actual attendance

## After the Event

Thank your attendees by:
- Posting in the plan chat
- Sending photos
- Sharing memories or feedback
