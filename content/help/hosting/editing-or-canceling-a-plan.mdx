---
title: Editing or Canceling a Plan
description: How to make changes to a plan or cancel it.
---

Plans can change. Here's how to edit or cancel one.

## Edit a Plan

1. Open the plan you want to edit
2. Click **Edit** or the pencil icon
3. Change any details:
   - Date, time, or location
   - Description or name
   - Attendee limit
   - Photo or cover image
4. Click **Save**

Everyone attending will be notified of changes automatically.

## Change the Time or Date

If you need to reschedule:
1. Click **Edit**
2. Select a new date and time
3. Save

People will get a notification about the reschedule.

## Cancel a Plan

1. Open the plan
2. Click **More options** or **...** menu
3. Select **Cancel Plan**
4. Add a cancellation message (explain why if helpful)
5. Confirm

Everyone who RSVPed will be notified that the plan is canceled. They won't lose any data—the plan just won't happen.

## Delete vs. Cancel

- **Cancel**: The plan stays visible in history but won't happen
- **Delete**: Permanently removes the plan (use sparingly)

Most of the time, canceling is better so people know what happened.
