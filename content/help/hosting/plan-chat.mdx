---
title: Using Plan Chat
description: Collaborate with attendees using the plan discussion thread.
---

Plan chat is a dedicated space for attendees to discuss a specific plan and coordinate details.

## Enable Chat on a Plan

When creating or editing a plan:
1. Click **Enable Chat** or similar option
2. Save the plan

Once enabled, everyone invited can chat.

## Send Messages

1. Open the plan
2. Click the **Chat** or **Discussion** tab
3. Type your message in the text box
4. Press Enter or click **Send**

## What to Chat About

Plan chat is great for:
- Asking questions about the event
- Coordinating carpools or timing
- Sharing photos or updates
- Discussing details like what to bring
- Confirming attendance details

## Notifications

By default, you'll get a notification when someone messages in your plan chat. You can disable this in your notification settings if it's too much.

## Only See Messages from Attendees

Plan chat is private to that plan. Only people who are invited or RSVPed can see the messages.

## After the Event

The chat stays there forever, so you can look back at memories, photos, or decisions made during planning.
