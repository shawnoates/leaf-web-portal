---
title: What is Friend Mode?
description: Recurring plans with your crew, on your schedule.
---

Friend Mode is for small groups (up to 15 people) who want to see each other regularly but never get around to planning it. Leaf does the planning: it picks a place, asks everyone which dates work, and locks the night.

You turn it on for a calendar you own, from the Friend Mode banner on the calendar's page.

## How it works

1. **You turn it on.** Nothing is sent yet. Friend Mode is on for your calendar and you're in.
2. **You invite your followers.** You see who gets an app notification and who gets a text, and nothing goes out until you confirm. People join with one tap or by replying IN. Anyone who doesn't join stays a follower, exactly as before.
3. **Leaf picks a place** from the group's shared list of spots. Anyone in the group can add places they've been wanting to try.
4. **Everyone picks dates.** Leaf offers two or three dates, and people tap the ones that work (or reply with the numbers).
5. **Leaf locks the night.** The date that works for enough people wins, and everyone gets a normal plan invite with Accept / Decline.
6. **Someone books it.** Whoever started that round gets the booking link. Leaf never books or pays for anything.
7. **Then it repeats** on the rhythm you set (every two weeks, monthly, and so on). Each person can also choose to be asked less often.

## Good to know

- People on the Leaf app get notifications in the app. Everyone else gets texts: at most 5 a week, and only between 9am and 9pm.
- Nobody's phone number is shown to anyone else in the group.
- Anyone can leave at any time: reply STOP, or turn it off in **Settings → Friend Mode** in the app. Leaving Friend Mode doesn't unfollow your calendar.
- You can turn Friend Mode off from the same switch. Anything being planned stops; past nights stay.
- It's free.
