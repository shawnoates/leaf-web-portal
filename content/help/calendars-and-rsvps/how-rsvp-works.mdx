---
title: How RSVP Works
description: Understand RSVPs and how to respond to invitations.
---

RSVP means "respond if you please" and it helps organizers know who's coming. Here's how it works in Leaf.

## The RSVP Options

When you see a plan, you can respond:

**Going**
- I plan to attend
- The organizer counts you as confirmed

**Maybe**
- I'm interested but not sure
- The organizer sees you as a possible attendee
- You can change to "Going" or "Not Going" later

**Not Going**
- I can't make it
- The organizer knows you won't be there
- This helps them finalize headcount

## How to RSVP

1. Open the plan
2. Click one of the RSVP buttons at the top
3. Your response is instant

You can always change your mind—just click a different option.

## Adding a Note

Some plans let you add details when you RSVP:
- How many guests you're bringing
- Dietary restrictions
- Special requirements
- A personal message

Click the note icon to add this info.

## What Organizers See

The host can see:
- How many people are going, maybe, or not going
- Names of everyone who RSVPed
- Any notes you provided
- Change history (if you update your RSVP)

## Reminders

Before a plan starts, you might get reminders:
- 1 week before
- 1 day before
- 1 hour before (if you want)

Adjust reminder settings in your account preferences.
