---
title: Syncing with Google Calendar
description: Add Leaf plans to your Google Calendar automatically.
---

Leaf plans can automatically sync to your Google Calendar, so all your events are in one place.

## Connect Google Calendar

1. Go to account settings
2. Click **Connected Accounts** or **Integrations**
3. Click **Connect Google Calendar**
4. Sign in with your Google account
5. Allow Leaf permission to access your calendar
6. Select which Leaf calendars to sync

Now your Leaf plans will appear in Google Calendar.

## How Sync Works

- New Leaf plans appear automatically in Google Calendar
- If you edit a plan in Leaf, Google Calendar updates within minutes
- If you edit a Leaf plan in Google Calendar, changes sync back to Leaf
- Cancelled plans are removed from both

## View in Google Calendar

1. Open Google Calendar at calendar.google.com
2. Look for a "Leaf" calendar in your sidebar
3. Toggle it on/off to show or hide Leaf plans
4. Plans show with all details (time, location, RSVP status)

## Disconnect Sync

If you want to stop syncing:
1. Go to account settings
2. Click **Connected Accounts**
3. Click **Disconnect Google Calendar**
4. All Leaf calendars will be removed from Google Calendar

Your Leaf calendars stay in Leaf—this just stops the two-way sync.

## Still Have Questions?

If plans aren't appearing in Google Calendar:
- Wait a few minutes for sync
- Check that you allowed Leaf to access your Google Calendar
- Try disconnecting and reconnecting
