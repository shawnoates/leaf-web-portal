Leaf sends notifications to keep you updated on plans, RSVPs, and messages. You control which ones you get.

## Types of Notifications

**Plan Updates**
- New plans created
- Plan time or location changed
- Plan canceled

**RSVP Notifications**
- Someone RSVPed to your plan
- Response count changed
- Close to event time

**Message Notifications**
- Someone replied in plan chat
- You were mentioned in a comment
- Direct messages

**Calendar Invitations**
- You've been invited to a calendar
- Someone joined your calendar

## Manage Notifications

1. Go to account settings
2. Click **Notifications**
3. Toggle each notification type on or off
4. Set quiet hours (don't notify between X and Y time)

You can customize by notification type—get messages but not plan updates, for example.

## Notification Channels

Notifications can come through:
- **Push notifications**: On your phone or browser
- **Email**: Digest or immediate
- **SMS**: Text message (if enabled)

Turn these on or off individually in settings.

## Quiet Hours

Set times when you don't want notifications:
- Example: "Don't notify between 9 PM and 8 AM"
- Choose which days this applies to
- Notifications pile up and show when quiet hours end

## Urgent Notifications

Some notifications (like plan cancellations) ignore quiet hours because they're important.

## Notification Badges

A badge on the Leaf app icon shows how many notifications you have. Clear them by opening the notifications section.
